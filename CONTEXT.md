# Kairos

Kairos is a product context for timing intelligence in B2B prospecting. The first product slice focuses on newly created businesses that may need digital presence services.

## Language

**Initial Customer Segment**:
Agencies and freelancers that sell websites, branding, SEO, or marketing services to newly created businesses in the United States.
_Avoid_: Generic outbound teams, broad B2B sales teams, all sales professionals

**Offered Service**:
The specific service the Kairos user sells, such as website development, branding, SEO, paid marketing, social media marketing, or e-commerce services.
_Avoid_: Generic segment, user category, broad sales intent

**Digital Need Signal**:
An observable sign that a newly created business may need help with website, branding, SEO, marketing, or online presence.
_Avoid_: Generic lead signal, raw company data, simple registration event

**Signal Discovery**:
The Kairos capability that finds and classifies digital need signals instead of requiring users to manually inspect each business.
_Avoid_: Manual research, simple filtering, generic enrichment

**Zero-Cost Signal Source**:
A public or no-cost source that Kairos can use to discover digital need signals without paid data providers or paid enrichment APIs.
_Avoid_: Paid enrichment, premium data provider, paid contact database

**National State Coverage**:
A U.S. coverage strategy that includes all 50 states while showing the quality and availability of each state's source coverage.
_Avoid_: Perfect nationwide data, hidden coverage gaps, arbitrary geography

**Timing Intelligence**:
A contextual reading of when a business is likely more receptive to a specific service based on its lifecycle and signals.
_Avoid_: Lead database, contact database, generic prospecting list

**Outreach Relevance**:
The moment when the user's offered service appears useful for a newly created business based on age, fit, digital need signals, and data confidence.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed purchase intent, conversion probability, generic lead quality

**Watched Business**:
A business that is valuable enough to keep monitoring because it matches a user's market, has meaningful signals, generated an alert, or was saved by the user.
_Avoid_: Every discovered business, permanent archive, generic account-owned record

**Contextual Outreach**:
A suggested first message that references the business context, timing stage, and relevant signal without encouraging mass outreach or guaranteed conversion claims.
_Avoid_: Spam copy, generic pitch, automated blast
